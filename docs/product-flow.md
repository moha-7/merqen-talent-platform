# Product Flow

## Candidate Side

1. Candidate browses published opportunities.
2. Candidate signs in or creates an account.
3. Candidate maintains a reusable profile.
4. Candidate applies to a job.
5. Job-specific screening responses are captured.
6. Candidate can track application status.

## Recruitment Side

1. Recruiter enters the tenant workspace.
2. Recruiter selects a business unit.
3. Applications are reviewed in the company workspace.
4. Candidate information, screening responses, files, and activity are available in one decision surface.
5. Valid stage transitions move the application through the recruitment workflow.
6. Notes and operational events remain attached to the application history.

## Job Lifecycle

Jobs support draft, published, paused, closed, and archived states.

Publishing readiness checks prevent incomplete jobs from being published for the first time.
